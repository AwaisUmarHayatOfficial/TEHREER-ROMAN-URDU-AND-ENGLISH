How do I get a Gemini API key?
  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and pick a project (or let it create a new one).
  3. Copy the key that appears and paste it above.

The key is free, but Google's quota limits apply. Long videos can take a while.

Your key is sent to Google's Gemini API only while the conversion runs. It is never saved or logged on our server. Gemini processes the video transcript. See the privacy policy and security page.

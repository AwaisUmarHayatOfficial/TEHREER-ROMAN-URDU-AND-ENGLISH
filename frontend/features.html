Features

Built to produce notes you can trust and study from, not a loose summary.

Two languages

Choose English or Roman Urdu before every conversion. English notes are clear, natural English even when the lecture is in Urdu or Hindi. Roman Urdu notes keep the speaker's words in Roman Urdu.

Either way, every sentence, example, number and warning is kept: nothing is summarised, merged or deleted.

Quality check

The output of every chunk is compared with its source by word count. Chunks that fall below the coverage threshold are retried or split into smaller parts automatically.

Structured notes

Headings, bullet lists, numbered steps and tables are created for you. Questions and answers, definitions and notes appear in colour-coded boxes.

Key terms, numbers, amounts and percentages are bolded, and the most important lines are highlighted so you can revise quickly.

Clickable timestamps

Each timestamp links to the exact second in the original video. The outline at the end lists every section with a link to the page and a link to the video.

Automatic branding

The header and tagline are generated from the channel name and the subject of the video, so every document looks like a proper course handout.

Resilient processing

If one Gemini model is rate limited, the next one is tried. Failed requests are retried after a short wait, and the job report tells you if any chunk could not be converted.

Live dashboard

See the percentage complete, which chunk is being converted, the elapsed time and the complete logs. You can cancel a job at any time.

Two download formats

A styled Word document (.docx) and a plain text copy (.txt), available from the page as soon as the job is done and from your account history for 24 hours.

Private by design

Your Gemini key is used for your job only and is never stored on our server. Finished files are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

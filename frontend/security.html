Security

What we do with your API key and your files, explained plainly.

Built so there is little to protect

The safest data is data we never keep. Your Gemini key is used for your conversion and then forgotten, and the files we generate are deleted automatically.

The sections below describe exactly how the service handles your information.

Your API key

The key is verified with Google, handed to the conversion process for your job only and replaced by asterisks if it ever appears in output. It is never written to disk or to our logs.

Encrypted in transit

The site is served over HTTPS, so your key, your video link and your downloads are encrypted between your browser and the server.

Isolated jobs

Every conversion runs in its own process with its own working folder. One user's job cannot read another's files.

Private files

Each job gets a long random identifier, and only the signed-in owner can open its status or download its files. There is no public listing of jobs or documents.

Automatic deletion

Finished files are deleted from the server after 24 hours, or immediately when you delete the conversion from your account.

Abuse protection

Input is validated (only YouTube video links are accepted), jobs are queued and rate limited per account and network, and a job that runs too long is stopped.

Strong password protection

Passwords are stored only as Argon2 hashes and must be long and complex. Accounts lock for a while after repeated wrong attempts, and every Gmail address is confirmed with an e-mailed code.

Protected sessions

You stay signed in with a random token in an HttpOnly cookie. Only a hash of it is stored, and you can sign out at any time.

Your data, your control

Delete any conversion, or your whole account with its history and files, from the Account page whenever you like.

What to keep in mind

  • The video transcript is sent to Google's Gemini API for conversion, so Google's terms apply to that processing.
  • Use a dedicated key. Create a separate API key for this tool and delete it at aistudio.google.com/apikey when you no longer need it.
  • The page sets standard security headers and does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Report a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security issue, please email support@tehreer.com with the details and steps to reproduce it. Please do not include real API keys in your report.

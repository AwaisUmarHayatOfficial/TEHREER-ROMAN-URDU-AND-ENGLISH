Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Tehreer handles when you use the notes tool, and what we do with it.

Your account

  • Account details. When you register we store your first and last name, username, phone number (not verified) and Gmail address. If you continue with Google, we receive your name, Gmail address, profile picture and Google account ID from Google.
  • Password. Stored only as a salted Argon2 hash, never in clear text. Accounts created with Google have no password unless you set one.
  • E-mail codes. Codes that confirm your Gmail address or reset your password are stored only as a one-way hash, expire after 10 minutes and are deleted after a day.
  • Sign-in records. For security we log sign-in events with the time, IP address and browser type, and keep them for up to 180 days.
  • Conversion history. For each conversion we keep the video link, video title, status and dates, for up to 90 days, so you can see your history. You can delete entries, or your whole account, at any time.

Information you provide

  • Gemini API key. You enter your own key so the conversion can run on your Google quota. It is used only for your job, passed to the conversion process and sent to Google's Gemini API. It is not written to disk or to our logs.
  • YouTube video link. We use it to fetch the video's title, channel name and subtitles.

Information we create

  • Generated files. The Word and text notes are stored on the server only so you can download them. They are deleted automatically after 24 hours, or earlier when you delete the conversion. Job logs are kept in the server's memory for the same period.
  • Technical data. Your IP address is held in the server's memory to limit abuse (for example the number of jobs per hour). Our web server may also keep standard access logs.

Third parties

  • Google Gemini. The video transcript is sent to Google's Gemini API using your key. Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.
  • YouTube. We retrieve public video information and subtitles from YouTube.
  • Google sign-in. If you choose Continue with Google, Google's sign-in service is used for that step, and Google's terms and privacy policy apply to it.
  • Google Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to download them.

Your browser

If you tick “Remember the key in this browser”, the key is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. We cannot read it from our server. Signing out removes it, and you can also untick the box and start a new job. The page also stores the ID of your current job in local storage so progress survives a refresh.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in (it is HttpOnly and only contains a random token). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Signing out removes it.

Your choices

You can delete any conversion, or your whole account with all its data, from the Account page, and files expire on their own after 24 hours. If you believe a key was exposed, revoke it at aistudio.google.com/apikey and create a new one.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
